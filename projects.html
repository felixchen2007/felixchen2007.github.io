Systems modeling

Rainwater harvesting

Developed an Excel-based model for a rainwater harvesting system serving a remote two-person household. The model considered water demand, rainfall, storage, pump behavior, and system-level tradeoffs.

My contribution

I organized assumptions, built spreadsheet calculations, interpreted pump and system behavior, and communicated results through visuals and presentation slides. Excel calculations helped compare design choices for water availability, reliability, cost, and maintainability.

What I learned

The design could not be judged by a single number. Balancing technical performance with stakeholder needs helped me understand how connected constraints shape an engineering decision.

Embedded systems

Arduino claw

Built and programmed an Arduino-controlled claw system using electrical components, wiring, and microcontroller logic. The goal was to control a physical mechanism through programmed input and output, and make its response reliable during testing.

My contribution

I connected the Arduino to the components, organized the wiring, debugged input and output behavior, and adjusted the code to make the mechanism respond as intended.

What I learned

A weak connection or small logic mistake could change the entire system response. Troubleshooting taught me to slow down, isolate problems, and verify each part of the system step by step.
