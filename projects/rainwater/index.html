Project 03 / Systems modeling

Rainwater Harvester

A rainwater collection and treatment system for a two-person, off-grid household in remote British Columbia. Designed around the whole system.

At a glance

Contribution
Six-person team
Tools
Excel / Simulation / Analysis
Discipline
Systems modeling

Design focus

  • Rainfall and demand modeling
  • Tank storage and collection
  • Filtration and disinfection
  • Cost and reliability tradeoffs

Balancing water, energy, and the realities of an off-grid household.

01

Modeling the household

Working in a six-person team, I contributed to an Excel simulation for rainwater harvesting and treatment for a two-person household in a remote British Columbia community. The model considered rainfall, water demand, storage, and energy.

02

Connecting the subsystems

The design brought together collection, pumping, filtration, and disinfection. We used pump and system curves to understand how components would operate together.

03

Making practical tradeoffs

We compared tank, piping, treatment, and power choices by cost, reliability, and maintenance needs. The objective was a system that made sense as a whole, within the constraints of off-grid living.
